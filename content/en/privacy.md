# Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy of PrimeBot on Discord and Telegram

PrimeBot (hereinafter referred to as "Bot") stores the following information:

- Registered teams on Telegram: Telegram group ID
- Registered teams on Discord: Server ID, Channel ID, Channel Name, Webhook ID, Webhook Token, Discord role
- Team information, such as the name or the personalization of notifications. You can find the Prime League's
  privacy policy [here](https://docs.gamesports.net/privacy/de).

If at any point you wish to have this data deleted, you can use the `/delete` command.

## Privacy Policy of the PrimeBot Website

1. It may happen that data and information of visitors and users (date, time, pages accessed) are stored via
   server access. We point out that no personal data (e.g. name, address, or email address) is stored without consent.
2. Should personal data be collected, we commit to obtaining the prior consent of the website user. We commit to
   not sharing data with third parties unless the visitor or user consents beforehand.
3. We point out that the transmission of data on the internet (e.g. via email) may have security vulnerabilities.
   Accordingly, flawless and uninterrupted protection of third-party data cannot be fully guaranteed. In this regard,
   our liability is excluded.
4. You have the right to information. You have the right at any time to receive complete and free information from
   us about your relevant data.
5. Furthermore, there is a right to correction/deletion of data/restriction of processing for the user.
